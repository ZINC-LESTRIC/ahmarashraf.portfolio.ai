About

Based in Sialkot — Pakistan's export capital — I spend my nights clearing shipments and my days building the tools that make trade smarter. I'm a Goods Declarant with hands-on experience in GD filing, HS classification, EFS, PSW/WeBOC, and export documentation. I also source and export sports goods, surgical instruments, and medical devices, connecting Pakistani manufacturers with international buyers. And when I'm not in customs, I'm coding — websites, full-stack apps, automation workflows, web scrapers, AI-integrated tools. If your problem sits at the intersection of trade and technology, I'm probably the right person.
